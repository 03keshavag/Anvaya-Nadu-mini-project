A LIVING DIGITAL RECORD

A Living Record of Karnataka’s Heritage

Karnataka is home to millennia of architectural evolution—from the early rock-cut caves of Vatapi to the delicate soapstone micro-sculptures of the Hoysalas and the colossal granite boulder shrines of Vijayanagara. Important monuments and architectural traditions deserve an engaging, accessible digital archive.

01

Architectural Preservation

Capturing the geometry, plinths, and carving traditions of historical monuments in interactive 3D space.

02

Geographic & Historical Discovery

Organizing heritage by authentic region, ruling dynasty, and architectural stone medium.

03

Cultural Grounding

Honoring verified historical contexts and indigenous terminology without fabricated claims.
